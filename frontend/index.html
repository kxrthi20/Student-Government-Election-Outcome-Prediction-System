About CART – Decision Tree Algorithm

CART (Primary Model)

  • 🔹 Algorithm: Classification and Regression Trees
  • 🔹 Criterion: Gini Impurity Index
  • 🔹 Max Depth: 5 levels
  • 🔹 Interpretable: Full tree visualization
  • 🔹 Library: scikit-learn

Random Forest (Comparison)

  • 🔸 Algorithm: Ensemble of Decision Trees
  • 🔸 Trees: 100 estimators
  • 🔸 Purpose: Accuracy comparison
  • 🔸 Feature Importance: Averaged across trees
  • 🔸 Library: scikit-learn
